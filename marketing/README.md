# Ecommerce Customer Intelligence & Predictive Growth Suite  

## Description:  
一個以電商顧客資料為基礎的 Customer Intelligence 專案，將 RFM 分群、顧客情緒分析、LTV 預測、Cohort Retention、Product Affinity 與 Business Recommendation Engine 整合成一套可支援行銷決策的分析流程。

This project analyzes e-commerce customer behavior and transforms raw customer, order, review, and event data into business-ready insights. It covers RFM segmentation, sentiment analysis, LTV prediction, cohort retention, product affinity, model validation, and a business recommendation engine to support data-driven marketing and customer growth decisions.

## Key Focus:
- Customer segmentation and behavior analysis
- LTV prediction and model validation
- Cohort retention and product affinity analysis
- Business recommendation engine for marketing actions
- Dashboard-ready CSV outputs for stakeholder reporting
